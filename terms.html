Wynne Plus subscription

  • The 10-minute day is free forever. Wynne Plus unlocks the 20- and 30-minute days.
  • Wynne Plus is offered monthly or yearly. The price is shown in the app before you confirm.
  • Each plan can include a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. If you don't cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the subscription starts and your Apple ID is charged.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel anytime in your iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID. Refunds are handled by Apple under its policies.

Not medical advice

Wynne offers general movement and relaxation suggestions, not medical advice. Move within a range that feels comfortable, stop if anything hurts, and check with a healthcare professional before starting a new routine, especially if you are pregnant, postpartum, injured, or managing a health condition.
