Privacy Policy

Wynne for iOS · Last updated: 19 September 2026

In short: Wynne keeps your check-ins, plan and history on your phone. There is no account, no tracking, no advertising and no analytics. Purchases of Wynne Plus are handled by Apple and by RevenueCat, our subscription service provider.

Who is responsible

Benneth Müller, Benneth Müller Digital Solutions & Education, Borgfelder Str. 16, 20537 Hamburg, Germany. Contact: ben@creaiter.com.

Data that stays on your device

Everything you enter in Wynne is stored only in the app's local storage on your iPhone and is never sent to us:

  • your setup answers (for example what shapes your day, your usual time window, areas to go easy on, optional cycle and midlife settings),
  • your daily check-ins (energy, time, body, optional period day),
  • your plans, finished or paused sessions and one-tap feedback,
  • your settings, such as language and reminder time.

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. We have no server that receives them. Deleting the app deletes all of this data. You can also reset it in the app under You.

Purchases (RevenueCat and Apple)

If you start a trial or subscribe to Wynne Plus, the payment is processed by Apple under your Apple ID. We never see your payment details. To check whether Wynne Plus is active, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA). RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID, your purchase and subscription status (transaction data from the App Store), and technical data such as app version, device model, operating system, country/storefront and IP address. This data is not linked to your name or email, and it is used only to provide and restore your subscription. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

Legal basis (GDPR): performance of the contract you enter into when you subscribe (Art. 6(1)(b)). RevenueCat processes data in the USA; the transfer is based on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or Standard Contractual Clauses.

Content updates

When the app starts, it may download one small text file with session text corrections from the same website that hosts this page (GitHub Pages). Like any web request, this reveals your IP address to the host for delivery; no identifier or personal data is sent. The app works fully without it.

What we do not do

  • No account or login.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, no advertising identifiers, no ads.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of data.
  • No Apple Health access.

Children

Wynne is not directed at children under 16 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete and port your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because your wellness data never leaves your phone, you control it directly. For data held by RevenueCat, contact us with your purchase details and we will help.

Health note

Wynne offers general movement and relaxation suggestions, not medical advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above.

Daten, die auf deinem Gerät bleiben

Alles, was du in Wynne eingibst, liegt ausschließlich im lokalen Speicher der App auf deinem iPhone und wird nie an uns übertragen:

  • deine Antworten aus der Einrichtung (zum Beispiel was deinen Tag prägt, dein übliches Zeitfenster, Bereiche, die geschont werden sollen, optionale Zyklus- und Lebensmitte-Einstellungen),
  • deine täglichen Check-ins (Energie, Zeit, Körper, optional Periodentag),

  • deine Einstellungen wie Sprache und Erinnerungszeit.

Erinnerungen sind lokale Mitteilungen, die auf deinem Telefon geplant werden. Wenn du die App löschst, sind alle diese Daten gelöscht. Du kannst sie auch in der App unter „Du" zurücksetzen.

Käufe (RevenueCat und Apple)

Wenn du eine Testphase startest oder Wynne Plus abonnierst, wickelt Apple die Zahlung über deine Apple-ID ab. Zahlungsdaten sehen wir nie. Diese Daten sind nicht mit deinem Namen oder deiner E-Mail verknüpft und dienen nur dazu, dein Abo bereitzustellen und wiederherzustellen. Siehe Datenschutzerklärung von RevenueCat und Datenschutzrichtlinie von Apple.

Rechtsgrundlage (DSGVO): Erfüllung des Vertrags, den du mit dem Abo abschließt (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b). RevenueCat verarbeitet Daten in den USA; die Übermittlung stützt sich auf das EU-US Data Privacy Framework und/oder Standardvertragsklauseln.
